## 3. Scenario Analysis & Seasonality Modeling
- **Three-Tier Scenarios**: Evaluates `Base Case` (historical baseline), `Upside` (+15% promotional boost), and `Downside` (-20% market contraction).
- **Seasonal Adjustments**: Incorporates Hajj/Umrah seasonal dates, Eid travel peaks, summer holiday surges, and university term intakes into forecasting calculations.
