# JOURNEY EXPERT LTD. — PATIENT CONSENT & PRIVACY GOVERNANCE
**Part 54 Execution • Autonomous Engineering System**

---

## 1. Explicit Consent Governance
The platform enforces explicit, granular consent prior to collecting, processing, or transmitting any medical records to partner hospitals or third-party service providers.

---

## 2. Consent Categories
- **Data Processing Consent**: Authorizes JEL to store diagnostic reports in the secure Document Vault.
- **Provider Sharing Consent**: Authorizes JEL to share specific medical reports with nominated partner hospitals for clinical evaluation and quote generation.
- **Travel Coordination Consent**: Authorizes JEL to share flight/mobility needs with airlines and local ground transfer operators (e.g. wheelchair assistance).
- **Communication Consent**: Authorizes case updates via WhatsApp, SMS, Email, or Patient Portal notifications.

---

## 3. Consent Management Rules
- **Consent Revocation**: Patients can withdraw consent at any time via the Patient Portal, halting record sharing with partner institutions.
- **Data Minimization**: Only reports relevant to the requested medical specialty are transmitted to evaluating hospitals.
- **Auditable Records**: All consent approvals and withdrawals are recorded with digital signatures, timestamps, and IP addresses.
