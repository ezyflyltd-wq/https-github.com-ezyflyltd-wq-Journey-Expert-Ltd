## 2. On-Ground Medical Support Features
- **Wheelchair & Mobility Assistance**: Pre-arranging airport wheelchair service (WCHR/WCHS/WCHC SSR codes) with airline ground teams.
- **Fit-to-Fly Certification**: Verification of airline medical clearance (MEDIF) for patients requiring oxygen or inflight medical monitoring.
- **Hospital Guest Houses & Hotels**: Curated inventory of long-stay apartments and hotels within 1-3 km of major medical centers (e.g., Apollo Hospitals Chennai/Delhi, Bumrungrad Bangkok, Mount Elizabeth Singapore).
