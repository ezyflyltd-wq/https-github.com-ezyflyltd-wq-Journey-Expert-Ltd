**Part 56 Execution • Autonomous Engineering System**

## 1. Claims Intake Workflow
The Claims Engine facilitates digital claim registration, evidence collection, missing document alerts, and real-time status tracking between travelers and insurer claims departments.

---

## 2. Claim Submission & Evidence Lifecycle
```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
|               Incident Report & Claim Registration                |
|   (Claim Type: Medical Expense, Flight Delay, Lost Baggage)       |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                  |
                                  v
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
|               Encrypted Evidence & Document Collection            |
| (Hospital Bills, Police Reports, PIR Form, Receipts, Passport)    |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                  |
                                  v
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
|               Direct Submission to Insurer Claims API             |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                  |
                                  v
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                   Insurer Adjudication & Decision                 |
|   [ APPROVED ]     [ ADDITIONAL_INFO_REQUIRED ]     [ REJECTED ]  |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                  |
                                  v
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                 Settlement & Payment Notification                 |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

---

## 3. Strict Non-Adjudication Rule
- **Insurer Decision Authority**: Claims decisions, payout amounts, or claim rejections are made exclusively by authorized insurer claims adjusters.
- **Audit Trails**: Every claim document upload, status change, and communication log is recorded with timestamps and access logs.
