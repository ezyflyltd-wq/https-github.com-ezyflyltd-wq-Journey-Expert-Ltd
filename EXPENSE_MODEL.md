# JOURNEY EXPERT LTD. — CORPORATE EXPENSE MANAGEMENT & RECEIPT OCR MODEL
**Part 57 Execution • Autonomous Engineering System**

---

## 1. Corporate Expense Management
Enables travelers to snap mobile receipt photos during business trips, extract expense items via OCR, and submit itemized expense reports directly to corporate finance.

---

## 2. Expense Submission & Approval Workflow
```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
|               Traveler Captures Receipt Image on Mobile           |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                  |
                                  v
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
|               OCR Text Extraction & Categorization                |
| (Merchant, Date, Amount, Currency, Expense Category - Meals/Taxi) |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                  |
                                  v
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
|               Cost Center & Expense Policy Check                  |
|    (Verifies daily meal caps, alcohol exclusion, mileage rates)   |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                  |
                                  v
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
|              Manager Approval & Reimbursement Export              |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

---

## 3. Expense Policy Caps & Duplicate Detection
- **Policy Caps**: Enforces meal caps (e.g., $50/day) and taxi receipt thresholds.
- **Duplicate Detection**: Hashes receipt image metrics and receipt numbers to prevent duplicate expense claims.
