Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Journey Expert Limited handles information submitted through this website and related service channels.

Information we collect

We may collect information you submit through enquiry, consultation, application, contact and support forms, such as your name, contact details and the service you request. We may also process limited technical data needed for security, analytics and website operation.

How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, deliver requested services, manage applications or bookings, improve service quality, prevent abuse and meet operational or legal obligations.

Sensitive documents

Do not send passport numbers, payment-card details or other highly sensitive information through public chat unless an authorized Journey Expert representative directs you to a secure channel.

AI assistant

Angela may process text or voice-derived requests to provide service information. AI responses are informational and may be handed off to a human when verification is needed.

Connected messaging and AI providers

When you contact us through Facebook Messenger, Instagram or WhatsApp, Meta processes information under its own policies. When automated replies are enabled, JEL may process your message text and channel identifiers to answer your enquiry. Message text may be sent to Google Gemini for generating an answer, and Cloudflare provides website and backend hosting. Please avoid sending sensitive documents in automated chat.

The Meta delivery-control database stores hashed message identifiers, delivery status and timestamps rather than message text or sender contact details. These delivery records are scheduled for removal after 30 days when subsequent deliveries run. Other business records and copies held by Meta or AI providers follow their applicable retention rules. See our data deletion instructions to request removal from JEL systems.

Sharing and retention

Information may be shared with service providers or institutions only when necessary for the service you request, subject to appropriate controls. We retain information only as long as reasonably needed for the stated purpose, compliance, security or dispute handling.

Your choices

You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information where applicable, subject to lawful retention requirements.
