## 1. End-to-End International Patient Lifecycle
The JEL Medical Travel module coordinates a 12-stage patient journey ensuring administrative accuracy, travel comfort, and patient care continuity:

1. **Lead Capture & Ingestion**: Digital inquiries from Web, WhatsApp, Partner Clinics, B2B Referral Agents, and Corporate Health Desks.
2. **Structured Medical Intake**: Registration of patient details, primary medical concern, preferred destination country (India, Thailand, Singapore, etc.), budget guidelines, and travel mobility needs.
3. **Encrypted Report Collection**: Patient uploads medical history, MRI/CT scans, blood panels, and current prescriptions into the Secure Medical Document Vault.
4. **Provider Inquiries & Quotations**: Dispatching anonymized/consented case files to 2-3 accredited partner hospitals for specialist clinical evaluation and treatment estimates.
5. **Itemized Quote & Options Review**: Presenting the patient with transparent treatment plans separating hospital costs, travel fares, accommodation, and local transport.
6. **Appointment & Hospital Confirmation**: Finalizing appointment date, doctor slot, and receiving the official Medical Visa Invitation Letter from the hospital.
7. **Medical Visa Facilitation**: Assisting patient and medical companion with medical visa applications, embassy appointments, and urgent travel clearances.
8. **Flight & Accommodation Booking**: Booking suitable flight itineraries with special assistance (wheelchair, stretcher, extra legroom) and hospital-adjacent accessible hotel rooms.
9. **Airport Meet & Greet & Transport**: Airport reception by multilingual JEL patient relation officer, wheelchair/ambulance transfer to hotel or hospital admission desk.
10. **In-Hospital Support & Interpretation**: Providing dedicated local language interpreters, daily coordinator check-ins, and companion assistance during hospital stay.
11. **Discharge & Medical Travel Clearance**: Reviewing fit-to-fly certificates, discharge summaries, medication prescriptions, and arranging airport departure transport.
12. **Post-Departure Follow-Up & Teleconsultation**: Scheduling post-treatment virtual follow-ups with treating specialists and local doctor handovers.
