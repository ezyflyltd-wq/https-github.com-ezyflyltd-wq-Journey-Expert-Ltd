## 1. Student Lifecycle Phases
The JEL Study Abroad platform manages a 10-stage student lifecycle from initial inquiry to post-arrival orientation:

1. **Inquiry & Lead Capture**: Multichannel ingestion from Website, Social Media, WhatsApp, Walk-ins, and Education Fairs.
2. **Counselling & Profiling**: Comprehensive assessment of academic transcripts, English proficiency (IELTS, PTE, TOEFL, Duolingo), budget limits, and target intakes.
3. **Course & University Selection**: Shortlisting target programs across 1,400+ partner universities based on entry requirements and career goals.
4. **Document Collection**: Secure upload and verification of academic certificates, SOPs, LORs, and passport copies.
5. **Application Submission**: Submitting applications to university admissions portals with real-time status tracking.
6. **Offer Management**: Evaluation of conditional and unconditional offer letters, scholarship awards, and deposit payments.
7. **CAS / I-20 / Attestation**: Collection of CAS (UK), PAL/Attestation Letter (Canada), I-20 (USA), or COE (Australia).
8. **Visa Case Processing**: Compilation of financial proof, TB testing, biometrics scheduling, DS-160/IRCC form completion, and visa submission.
9. **Pre-Departure & Travel**: Flight booking with student baggage allowance, travel insurance, and currency exchange.
10. **Post-Arrival Support**: Airport pickup, temporary student accommodation, local bank account setup, and university registration guidance.

## 2. Student Lead Scoring Model
- **Academic Fit Score**: Evaluates GPA against target country admission baselines.
- **English Readiness Score**: Maps test scores to minimum band requirements.
- **Financial Capability Score**: Assesses sponsor funding against tuition and living expense benchmarks.
- **Urgency & Intake Timing**: Prioritizes upcoming intake deadlines (e.g., September Fall Intake, January Spring Intake).
