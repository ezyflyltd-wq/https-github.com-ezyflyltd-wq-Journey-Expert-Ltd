# JOURNEY EXPERT LTD. — HOTEL ROOM ALLOCATION & ROOMING LIST MODEL
**Part 53 Execution • Autonomous Engineering System**

---

## 1. Rooming List Management
Religious travel accommodation requires complex room allocation logic balancing gender segregation, family groupings, room occupancy types (Single, Double, Triple, Quad, Sharing), and special mobility requests.

---

## 2. Room Allocation Workflow
1. **Contract Room Block**: System reserves bulk room blocks at Makkah/Madinah hotels for specific date ranges.
2. **Pilgrim Preference Input**: Pilgrims specify rooming preference (Quad Sharing, Family Private Room, Single Bed).
3. **Automated Matching Engine**: Groups pilgrims by gender and family units into allocated rooms while maintaining target room occupancy.
4. **Voucher & Hotel Export**: Generates official hotel rooming lists formatted for hotel front desk check-in systems.

---

## 3. Rooming Constraints
- **Gender Segregation Rules**: Unrelated male and female pilgrims are automatically segregated in shared rooms.
- **Mahram & Family Integrity**: Families are kept in contiguous or private multi-bed rooms (Double, Triple, Quad, Executive Suite).
