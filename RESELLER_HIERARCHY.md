# JOURNEY EXPERT LTD. — RESELLER HIERARCHY & SUB-AGENT MODEL
**Part 58 Execution • Autonomous Engineering System**

---

## 1. Multi-Level Distribution Hierarchy
Supports multi-level agent distribution hierarchies (`JEL Master Platform` -> `Master Travel Agency` -> `Sub-Agent` -> `Local Reseller`).

---

## 2. Parent-Child Governance
- **Sub-Agent Limits**: Master agencies can set credit limits, markup ceilings, product access restrictions, and user permissions for their sub-agents.
- **Data Isolation Enforcement**: Sub-agents view only their own bookings and customers. Master agencies view aggregated sub-agent reports without cross-tenant data leakage.
