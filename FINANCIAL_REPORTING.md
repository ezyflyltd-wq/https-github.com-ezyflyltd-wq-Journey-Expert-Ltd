# JOURNEY EXPERT LTD. — CFO FINANCIAL REPORTING & AUDIT SUITE
**Part 62 Execution • Autonomous Engineering System**

---

## 1. Executive Financial Reporting Suite
Generates authoritative financial statements and audit schedules for executives, board members, auditors, and tax authorities:

1. **Profit & Loss Statement (P&L)**: Gross booking value, recognized revenue, cost of sales, gross profit, operating expenses, EBITDA, and net profit.
2. **Balance Sheet**: Assets (cash, receivables, gateway float), liabilities (unearned customer revenue, supplier payables, B2B wallet liabilities), and retained equity.
3. **Statement of Cash Flows**: Operating cash flow, investing cash flow, and financing activities.
4. **Accounts Receivable (AR) Aging**: Outstanding corporate credit lines and B2B agent balances (0-30 days, 31-60 days, 60+ days overdue).
5. **Accounts Payable (AP) Schedule**: Outstanding supplier settlements categorized by airline, hotel chain, and DMC.

## 2. Statutory Tax & Audit Compliance
- **Multi-Jurisdiction Tax Engine**: Calculates local VAT, AIT (Advance Income Tax), and withholding tax schedules based on transaction rules.
- **Immutable Audit Trail**: Every posted journal entry, payment capture, refund approval, and FX adjustment retains complete timestamped user and system provenance.
