# JOURNEY EXPERT LTD. — HALAL TOURISM & MUSLIM-FRIENDLY TRAVEL MODEL
**Part 53 Execution • Autonomous Engineering System**

---

## 1. Halal Tourism Model
The Halal Tourism module extends Journey Expert Ltd.'s leisure travel offerings for Muslim families and travelers seeking verified Muslim-friendly amenities worldwide.

---

## 2. Core Halal Travel Attributes
- **Halal Dining Assurance**: Verified Halal-certified restaurants or Muslim-owned dining options.
- **Prayer Facilities & Qibla Direction**: Hotel rooms equipped with Qibla indicators, prayer mats, and proximity to local mosques.
- **Alcohol-Free & Family Environment**: Option to filter alcohol-free hotels, private pool villas, and family-friendly beach resorts.
- **Islamic Heritage Itineraries**: Curated tours showcasing Islamic history and cultural heritage (e.g. Istanbul & Ottoman Heritage, Andalusia & Moorish Spain, Tashkent & Samarkand, Cairo & Al-Azhar, Sarajevo & Balkans).

---

## 3. Verification & Transparency
- **Halal Verification Labels**: Displays clear verification badges (`Halal Certified Kitchen`, `Muslim-Owned`, `Halal Food Available on Request`).
- **No False Claims Policy**: Amenities are verified through direct hotel contracts or DMC ground partners prior to listing.
