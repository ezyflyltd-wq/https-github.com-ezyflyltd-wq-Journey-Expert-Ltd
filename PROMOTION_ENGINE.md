## 2. Fair Pricing & Disclosure Standards
- **No Deceptive Pricing**: Promotional discounts are calculated against real historical base rates. Fake claims (e.g. false "Lowest Price Guaranteed" without real-time benchmark) are strictly forbidden.
- **Auditable Campaign Rules**: Every campaign defines start/end timestamps, max budget caps, and max usage counts per customer segment.
