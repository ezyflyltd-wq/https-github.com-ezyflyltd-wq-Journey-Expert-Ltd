## 1. AI Business Travel Assistant Architecture
Assists corporate travelers and bookers with flight options, corporate policy queries, itinerary compilation, expense report preparation, and emergency travel assistance.

---

## 2. Policy Enforcement & Revalidation Rules
- **Strict Corporate Policy Compliance**: The AI evaluates options directly against the corporate account policy matrix (e.g. *"Flight options on Saudia and Emirates match your company's $800 cap; British Airways option exceeds limit by $150 and requires manager approval"*).
- **Zero Hallucinated Approvals**: The AI assistant never approves financial bookings or policy exceptions without executing formal approval workflows.
- **Live Price Revalidation**: All flight and hotel recommendations are revalidated against live GDS/NDC inventory before request submission.
