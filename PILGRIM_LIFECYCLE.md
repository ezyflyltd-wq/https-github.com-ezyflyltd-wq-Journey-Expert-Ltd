## 1. Pilgrim Lifecycle Overview
The JEL Religious Travel module manages a 12-stage pilgrim lifecycle ensuring high-touch operational support before, during, and after pilgrimage:

1. **Lead & Inquiry Capture**: B2C web inquiries, B2B agent referrals, corporate group requests, and walk-in registrations.
2. **Pilgrim Intake & Registration**: Registration of passport details, emergency contact, dietary/wheelchair assistance, and family relationship details.
3. **Package Customization**: Selection of Hajj (Shifting/Non-Shifting), Umrah (Economy, VIP 5-Star, Ramadan), or Halal Vacation packages.
4. **Document Vault Storage**: Upload and verification of passport copies, digital photos, vaccination certificates, and Mahram relationship proof.
5. **Installment Payment Plan**: Multi-tranche payment tracking (Deposit, Flight Allocation, Final Balance) linked to central financial ledger.
6. **Visa & Nusuk Permit Submission**: Automated compilation for Saudi Umrah visa issuance and Rawdah Mubarak permit scheduling via Nusuk.
7. **Flight PNR Allocation**: Group seat assignments on Saudia, Biman Bangladesh, Emirates, Flynas, or Gulf Air charter flights.
8. **Hotel Rooming List Allocation**: Rooming assignment (Quad, Triple, Double, Single, Family Suite) across Makkah and Madinah hotels.
9. **Pre-Departure Orientation**: Digital briefing packages, packing lists, Ihram instructions, and group leader introduction.
10. **In-Transit & Ground Operations**: Airport Meet & Greet, VIP bus transport, hotel check-in, and scheduled Ziyarat tours.
11. **On-Ground Guidance & Emergency Dispatch**: Real-time group leader check-ins, guide support, and 24/7 hotline assistance.
12. **Return & Post-Trip Follow-up**: Post-trip feedback, luggage tracking assistance, and loyalty rewards registration.
