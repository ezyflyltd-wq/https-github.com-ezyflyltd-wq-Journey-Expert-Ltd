# JOURNEY EXPERT LTD. — PILGRIM DOCUMENT VAULT SPECIFICATION
**Part 53 Execution • Autonomous Engineering System**

---

## 1. Document Vault Architecture
The Religious Travel Document Vault securely stores passports, visa approvals, vaccination records, Mahram affidavits, and Nusuk permit slips with strict access control and encryption.

---

## 2. Document Types
1. **Passport Copy**: Minimum 6-month validity check from departure date.
2. **Umrah / Hajj Visa**: Electronic visa PDF issuance from Saudi Ministry portal.
3. **Nusuk Permits**: Rawdah Mubarak permit (Masjid An-Nabawi) and Umrah slot permit barcodes.
4. **Health & Vaccination Certificates**: Meningococcal meningitis vaccination and mandatory health clearances.
5. **Mahram Evidence**: Relationship proof (Marriage Certificate, Birth Certificate) for female pilgrims travelling with Mahram.
6. **Flight & Hotel Vouchers**: E-tickets and hotel confirmation vouchers.

---

## 3. Privacy & Compliance Rules
- **AES-256 Storage Encryption**: Passport images and visa documents are stored privately with short-lived signed URL access.
- **Role-Based Access Control**: B2B agents view only their registered pilgrims; Operations staff access active group manifests.
