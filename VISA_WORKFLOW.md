## 2. Country-Specific Immigration Requirements
- **United Kingdom (Student Visa - Points Based System)**: 50 points for CAS, 10 points for Financial Requirement (28-day bank holding rule), 10 points for English Language requirement.
- **Canada (Study Permit - SDS & Non-SDS)**: Letter of Acceptance (LOA), Provincial Attestation Letter (PAL), Guaranteed Investment Certificate (GIC $20,635 CAD), 1-year tuition payment proof.
- **Australia (Subclass 500 Student Visa)**: Genuine Student (GS) assessment, Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC), financial capacity proof ($29,710 AUD/year).
- **United States (F-1 Student Visa)**: Form I-20, SEVIS I-901 fee payment ($350), DS-160 submission, US Embassy visa interview preparation.
