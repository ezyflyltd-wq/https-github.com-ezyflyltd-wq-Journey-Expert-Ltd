## 2. Medical Visa Requirements
- **Hospital Visa Invitation Letter**: Contains patient name, passport number, companion details, attending doctor, department, and expected stay duration.
- **Embassy Attestation & Biometrics**: Coordinates priority appointment slots at embassy visa centers (e.g. Indian Visa Application Center - IVAC, Thai Embassy, Turkish Embassy).
- **Attending Companion Clearance**: Includes designated medical companion on hospital letterhead for joint visa processing.
