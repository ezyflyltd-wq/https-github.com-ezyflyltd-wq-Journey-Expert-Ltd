## 3. Responsible AI Guardrails
- **No Admissions / Visa Guarantees**: Every recommendation includes a disclaimer emphasizing that admission and visa decisions are subject to official university and immigration review.
- **Source-Grounded Responses**: Immigration rules (bank holding periods, post-study work rights) cite official UK Home Office, IRCC, or Australian Home Affairs documentation dates.
- **Human Escalation**: Complex profile queries (e.g. study gaps over 5 years, previous visa refusals) automatically generate a consultation request for senior human visa officers.
