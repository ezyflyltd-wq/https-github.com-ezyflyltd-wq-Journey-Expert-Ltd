## 1. Executive Summary
Journey Expert Ltd. / JEL Study Abroad provides an end-to-end digital student recruitment, admissions, and visa consultancy ecosystem.
The platform unifies Lead CRM, AI-powered Course Matching, Document Vault, University Application Tracker, Visa Case Management, and Post-Arrival Travel Integration across primary destination countries (UK, Canada, Australia, USA, Ireland, New Zealand, Schengen).

## 3. Core Principles
- **Source-Backed Immigration Compliance**: All visa checklists, financial proof thresholds, and work rights rules are versioned and linked to official government sources (UK Home Office, IRCC Canada, Australian Home Affairs, US Department of State).
- **Zero Guarantees Rule**: System and AI Counselors strictly state eligibility and offer possibilities without making unsubstantiated guarantees regarding university admissions, scholarships, or visa approvals.
- **Data Privacy & Vault Security**: Student documents (transcripts, passports, bank statements) are stored with AES-256 encryption and restricted by strict role-based access controls (RBAC).
