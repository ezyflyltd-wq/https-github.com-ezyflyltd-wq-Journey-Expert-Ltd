# JOURNEY EXPERT LTD. — GROUND TRANSPORTATION & FLEET MODEL
**Part 53 Execution • Autonomous Engineering System**

---

## 1. Ground Transport Management
Coordinates intercity and airport transfers between Jeddah Airport (JED), Makkah, Madinah, and Ziyarat historical sites (Cave of Hira, Mount Arafat, Mina, Muzdalifah, Masjid Quba, Mount Uhud).

---

## 2. Fleet & Route Hierarchy
- **Jeddah Airport -> Makkah Hotel**: VIP Bus / Coaster / Private GMC Transfer.
- **Makkah -> Madinah Sector**: Haramain High-Speed Rail seat tickets or VIP Coaster / Bus.
- **Ziyarat Guided Tours**: Dedicated group bus with local religious guide and historical itinerary.
- **Madinah Hotel -> Prince Mohammad Bin Abdulaziz Airport (MED)**: Departure transfer.

---

## 3. Bus Manifest & Dispatching
- **Bus Allocation Engine**: Assigns pilgrims to specific Bus Numbers (`Bus 01`, `Bus 02`) with designated seats and bus captains.
- **Driver & Guide Dispatch**: Real-time GPS location tracking and contact dispatch for bus captains and ground coordinators.
