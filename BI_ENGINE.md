## 1. BI Engine Architecture
Provides interactive, role-tailored BI dashboards with real-time updates, drill-down explorations (Enterprise -> Country -> Destination -> Product -> Transaction), and multi-format exports (CSV, PDF, Excel).

## 3. Interactive Features & Drill-Through
- **Global Filters**: Date ranges, countries, products, channels, currencies, and customer segments.
- **Drill-Through Access**: Authorized executives can click on aggregate figures to inspect underlying individual booking and payment records.
