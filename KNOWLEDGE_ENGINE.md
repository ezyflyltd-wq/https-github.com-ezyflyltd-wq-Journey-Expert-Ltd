# JOURNEY EXPERT AI — PROPRIETARY KNOWLEDGE BASE MODEL
**Part 60 Execution • Autonomous Engineering System**

---

## 1. Centralized Knowledge Base Architecture
Stores verified company SOPs, carrier fare rules, hotel policies, destination guidebooks, visa documentation rules, and insurance policy wordings.

---

## 2. Knowledge Source Hierarchy
1. **Official JEL APIs & GDS/NDC Data**: Live fares, hotel availability, seat maps.
2. **Carrier & Insurance Policy Wordings**: Official carrier rules and insurance coverage schedules.
3. **JEL Verified Destination Guides**: Curated attractions, visa rules, and local transport advice.
4. **General AI Knowledge**: General travel knowledge (used only when verified sources are unavailable).

---

## 3. Knowledge Freshness & Expiry
- Dynamic knowledge items (e.g., visa entry rules, airline baggage limits) carry expiry timestamps. Outdated knowledge entries trigger automated re-verification alerts.
