# JOURNEY EXPERT LTD. — AI MEDICAL TRAVEL ASSISTANT SAFETY & GUARDRAILS
**Part 54 Execution • Autonomous Engineering System**

---

## 1. AI Safety & Responsibility Governance
The AI Medical Travel Assistant is strictly limited to administrative navigation, travel logistics, document checklists, and hospital directory search.

---

## 2. Hard Disclaimers & Operational Boundaries
- **No Clinical Diagnosis or Advice**: The AI assistant strictly declines clinical diagnostic queries (e.g. *"What is causing my chest pain?"* or *"What dosage of medication should I take?"*).
- **Mandatory Escalation Trigger**: Any query containing symptom reports, urgent medical distress, or treatment advice request automatically triggers:
  1. Mandatory Display: *"Journey Expert Ltd. provides administrative travel coordination only and cannot offer medical advice. Please consult a qualified doctor or contact local emergency services immediately."*
  2. One-click routing to the hospital IPC or JEL human case coordinator.
- **No Treatment or Outcome Guarantees**: The AI never promises cure rates, surgical success probabilities, or fixed final treatment costs.
- **Transparent Estimate Labelling**: All displayed cost ranges are explicitly labelled as *"Estimated hospital quotes subject to in-person clinical evaluation by treating physicians"*.
