## 1. Specialist Domain Agent Roster
The Agent Registry defines system prompts, capabilities, tool bindings, and operational constraints for each specialist domain agent:

1. **Travel Search Agent**: Executes real-time flight, hotel, tour, transfer, and insurance availability queries across live GDS/NDC and partner APIs.
2. **Travel Planning Agent**: Builds day-by-day itineraries, verifies geographic feasibility, and calculates budget breakdowns.
3. **Flight Agent**: Explains fare rules, baggage allowances, cabin class amenities, and schedule alternatives.
4. **Hotel Agent**: Compares room types, meal plans, cancellation policies, and hotel location ratings.
5. **Tour & DMC Agent**: Coordinates guided excursions, multi-day packages, vehicle capacities, and guide rosters.
6. **Insurance Agent**: Explains policy terms, coverage categories, deductibles, and claim submission steps without making unauthorized coverage guarantees.
7. **Visa Agent**: Generates document checklists, explains embassy visa steps, and tracks application statuses without promising visa approvals.
8. **Study Abroad Agent**: Compares university rankings, admission requirements, tuition fees, and CAS/I-20 visa steps.
9. **Corporate Policy Agent**: Evaluates travel requests against corporate policy rules and routes out-of-policy items to managers for approval.
10. **Hajj & Umrah Agent**: Manages pilgrim itineraries, Makkah/Madinah hotel proximity, and Rawdah permit guidance.
11. **Medical Tourism Agent**: Guides patients through partner hospital directories, administrative travel coordination, and companion lodging.
12. **Customer Support Agent**: Handles FAQs, booking status checks, and escalates complex disputes to human support teams.
