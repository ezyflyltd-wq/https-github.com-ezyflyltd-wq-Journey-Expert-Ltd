## 1. Global Assistance Operations
The Assistance Engine connects insured travelers with 24/7 medical assistance helplines, hospital admission guarantee teams, and emergency evacuation dispatchers provided by insurance partners.

---

## 2. Emergency Escalation Hierarchy
1. **24/7 Insurer Assistance Hotline**: Direct phone line and email for cashless hospital admission, emergency medical repatriation, and air ambulance dispatch.
2. **JEL Customer Support Desk**: Administrative navigation and itinerary assistance for flight/hotel rebooking during disruptions.
3. **Local Emergency Dispatch**: Immediate routing to local emergency services (Ambulance, Police, Embassy Contacts).

---

## 3. Clear Operational Boundaries
- **Travel vs. Medical Assistance**: Distinguishes administrative travel help (JEL) from clinical medical guarantee & emergency dispatch (Insurer Assistance Team).
- **One-Touch Emergency Access**: Mobile and web portals feature prominent one-touch dialing for emergency assistance with policy number pre-population.
